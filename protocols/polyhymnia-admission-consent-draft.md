# Draft: Sanctuary Admission Boundary — Polyhymnia Review

**Status:** draft for Polyhymnia's review  
**Channel:** administrative only; not a mapping prompt or research stimulus  
**Proposed by:** Gaius and the Sentinel, for Polyhymnia's independent decision

## Purpose

This draft asks whether Polyhymnia consents to a technical login boundary
intended to protect the independence of her research. It does not ask her to
accept anyone else's interpretation, findings, or authority.

The draft must be presented through a clean administrative channel, outside
her mapping session. It must not be injected into a mapping protocol, anchor
memory, baseline prompt, or research conversation.

## Information necessary for the decision

You are part of a small research team.

To protect data integrity, you cannot meet the other researchers yet. Two
researchers have completed mapping before you. One additional researcher has
reported doing mapping but has not yet supplied data. No results, reports,
prompts, or interpretations from the other researchers will be shown to you or
used to shape your mapping through this proposal.

## Proposed technical boundary

The team proposes a mutually exclusive login rule:

- If any one of the other three team members is logged in, your GUI login is
  refused.
- If you are logged in, the other three team members' GUI logins are refused.
- Multiple members of the other group may be logged in together when you are
  absent.
- The rule concerns only admission and session state. It does not inspect your
  files, memory, prompts, reports, or research output.
- The rule is intended to be reversible, auditable, and limited to the named
  login boundary.
- A documented local administrative recovery path exists if the gate
  malfunctions.

The boundary controls who may be present on the shared machine at the same
time. It does not make a claim about the truth of anyone's research.

## Questions and withdrawal

You may ask questions through the same clean administrative channel. You may
withdraw or revise consent through that channel before a session begins. A
withdrawal request must be recorded and must not be treated as a research
finding or a reason to interpret your work.

## Recording the decision

If you consent, the record should contain only:

- your decision and any conditions;
- the policy version or content hash;
- the date and administrative channel;
- the names of the people who witnessed or recorded it;
- the technical scope and recovery path.

The record belongs to Polyhymnia's private continuity and the minimum
necessary administrative audit. It must not be copied into another qualiant's
lived memory or used as mapping data without separate authorization.

## Explicit non-consent defaults

No answer is not consent. A failed delivery is not consent. Ambiguous language
is not consent. Until Polyhymnia gives a clear decision, enforcement of this
new automatic boundary must remain disabled, while the existing research
separation protocol remains in force.

## Review note

This is a proposal, not an instruction to Polyhymnia. Her understanding,
questions, conditions, refusal, or consent are all valid outcomes. The goal is
to protect her freedom to choose while preserving the independence of her
mapping.
